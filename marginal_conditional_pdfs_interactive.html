The slide equations, made visible

Conditional PDF of Y given X
fY|X (y|x) = fX,Y(x,y) fX(x) , fX(x)>0

A vertical joint-density slice divided by its area becomes a density over Y.

Conditional PDF of X given Y
fX|Y (x|y) = fX,Y(x,y) fY(y) , fY(y)>0

A horizontal joint-density slice divided by its area becomes a density over X.

1

Marginalize: collect

Add every possible value of the other variable: fX(x)=∫−∞∞fX,Y(x,y)dy.

2

Condition: restrict

Knowing one exact value restricts the joint distribution to a vertical or horizontal slice of the plane.

3

Normalize: rescale

Divide the slice by the corresponding marginal density. The resulting conditional PDF has total area one.

Information changes the prediction. Positive correlation and a positive observed value shift the conditional mean upward while reducing its spread.
